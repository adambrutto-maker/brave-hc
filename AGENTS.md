## Statistical authority and nonnegotiable invariants

1. **Official records are actuals.** Keep original recorded goals, assists, points, goalkeeper outcomes, career totals, game-by-game events, dates and Summer/Winter records as published by the verified HTO source or their separately approved record system. Do not rewrite, combine or re-rank official record books with normalized, projected, or estimated statistics.
2. **Analytics are derived and separately labeled.** Show Actual, Winter-equivalent, Summer-equivalent, or explicitly identified league-defined comparison views. Equivalent values can be fractional; never count them as actual goals, milestones, award wins or game results.
3. **Both directions are supported.** The verified Brave H.C. workbook uses Summer 48-minute regulation games and Winter 32-minute regulation games. Summer to Winter: multiply by 32/48. Winter to Summer: multiply by 48/32. Generic formula: original counted production × (target scheduled regulation minutes / source scheduled regulation minutes). This does not adjust games played.
4. **Do not confuse PPG metrics.** The workbook's field named PPG is power-play goals, **not** points per game. Points per game is adjusted points / actual GP where GP > 0. No rate is shown for GP = 0.
5. **Source formats must be verified per period.** The manager may configure league, schedule/game duration and period minutes. Save versioned historical season/game-type format assignments. Changing the current league settings must not silently rewrite historical conditions. Do not infer historic playing minutes solely from a label Summer or Winter; suppress equivalent views until the source mapping is verified.
6. **Regular time vs season length are separate axes.** A game-duration equivalent is not a 10-game Summer to 23-game Winter full-season projection. If season-length scenarios are later supported, label them explicitly as hypothetical projections, never official or default.
7. **Goalkeeper safety.** Game-length-equivalent goals against is an analytical measure. Do not convert wins, losses, ties, shutouts, save percentage, or official GAA by a skater scoring formula without a separately verified definition and source data. Missing metrics are unknown, not zero.
8. **Separation of sources.** Keep HTO current-season feed, historical career archive, authoritative workbook and derived analytics separately dated and reconciled. Never add a newer seasonal total onto an older career archive without verified source reconciliation.
9. **Identity and security.** Use verified official HTO player IDs. Never publish unpublished lineup instructions, private player feedback, individual coaching notes or unlicensed video in the public static GitHub Pages repository. Game-specific coaching is accessible only through an authenticated, approved player/team playbook tied to confirmed attendance, actual lineup role, season status and coach approval.
10. **Bio presentation.** Align the player Bio with the polished Milestone Highlights header and layout; emphasize advanced visualizations rather than the native Stats table. Use simple in-place buttons: All, Production, Trends, Goaltending (where relevant) and Coaching. The main skater chart ALWAYS shows raw earned goals and assists as stacked bars; ONLY the points-per-game line switches between No adjustment (actual), Winter-adjusted and Summer-adjusted. Source and conversion context must remain visible. A season with unverified normalization mapping retains raw bars while holding that season's adjusted line. The player Bio has its OWN native, polished current-season projection component from the exact same widgets/projection.json model as the homepage: never a homepage iframe, link-only substitute, or separately recalculated forecast. Preserve model provisional status. Defer homepage styling until after the Bio.
11. **Publication gates.** Propose code through a reviewable PR, run source-only tests and mobile QA. Do not claim a draft module or PR is wired into the live Bio. Do not alter native HTO editor content, the Master Workbook, AwardsScoring, official Records or public coaching access without a separate authorized release and evidence.


12. **Read-only historical duration mapping is now source-verified.** The original Master Workbook SHA-256 0002fd9f06596af8d0a00fc1b53a618ef15479f941ad5c1e6265b27002a65154 was read without modification. Its Master Schedule maps all **18/18** HTO career season labels to S/W season codes and 2/3 or 3/2 conversion factors. The exact mappings are in widgets/league-game-formats.json with game type scope ALL_HTO_GAME_TYPES. Rate comparisons are safe; this does **not** certify complete game-by-game HTO-vs-workbook player stat parity. New/exception seasons remain held. See docs/261028_Bio_Historical_Duration_Mapping_Evidence.md.
13. **New goalie projection stays separate and gated.** Goalie forecasts build on skater-model historical attendance and increasing within-season current-evidence principles but form an independent probabilistic pilot. Projected appearances, wins and goals-against-per-appearance require source-matched historical team season denominators and current goalie outcome evidence; never invent future starts or save percentage. Do not show unsupported numerical projections. Opponent strength is descriptive and is NOT a win-probability coefficient until independently validated, with zero-history opponents falling back to a documented neutral baseline. Current skater forecast stays a faithful read of widgets/projection.json until the opponent-adjusted extension passes its own QA.

Reference implementation: widgets/player-performance-normalization.js and widgets/league-game-formats.json. Safety tests: node --test tests/test_player_performance_normalization.cjs.

## Highlights leadership-source completeness

The native HTO Highlights ledger does not contain every previously published captain and assistant-captain service history. The separately verified widgets/player-bio-leadership.json contains thirteen source-documented tenures across nine players. Dylan Brennan (#525407) is documented as Team Captain 2018–2019, independently corroborated by the public September 17, 2018 team bulletin. The user reported his missing captain history in Highlights.

Show service history in its own Team Leadership History section within Honors, NOT as extra rows in the original Complete Achievement Timeline or counted as added awards. Any Highlights presentation must reconcile the public Bio leadership file under strict matching official player IDs, names, role wording, dates, source status, provenance and duplicate checks. Never rewrite native HTO Highlights records, infer an election or award from a tenure, or turn player-coach service into a captain badge. Native Elected Captain rows and recorded Captain tenure are distinct events. Two historical assistant-captain statements have founder-confirmed provenance; they must not be falsely called native HTO awards.

Use the presentation-only widgets/player-highlights-leadership-parity.js and run source-negative and live-equivalent browser tests before publishing. Missing/tampered leadership sources must not fabricate Captain records, and the original native ledger must remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Full rationale: docs/261028_Highlights_Leadership_History_Parity.md.
