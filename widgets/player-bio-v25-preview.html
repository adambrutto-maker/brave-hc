Brave H.C. 1-2-2 team framework

Braves team system · 1-2-2

Protect the middle. Force the puck carrier outside. Maintain coverage before attacking together after the turnover.

  • F1: Guide the puck carrier toward the boards.
  • F2 and F3: Protect your lanes and close the middle.
  • Defence: Keep the middle covered and support behind the forwards.

General team framework. Game-night roles are set by the coach.

Game-specific roles require coach verification.
