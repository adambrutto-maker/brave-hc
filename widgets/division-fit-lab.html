Methodology, source boundaries and exclusions

The current season index uses verified reciprocal games, smoothed goal and W/L/T performance, schedule difficulty and independently qualified prior-winter results where supported. It is not a calibrated ranking or win probability. Alternative grouping layouts are read-only arithmetic exercises; no division changes are made. Other leagues and placeholder categories are outside this adult Sunday division comparison. Historical Brave H.C. game matches prove opponents and scores in a specific season, not roster continuity in 2026. No same-named older team is automatically credited with current strength. Four accepted weeks, at least ten games for 75% of the adult pool and independent cross-division fixtures are minimum evidence thresholds; none substitutes for human review.

Sources: Official Sunday Winter 2026/27 standings · Verified HockeyOS League Intelligence. Source and methodology are date-stamped in the copied report. No original HTO, Sports Village, goalie or Master Workbook records are modified.
