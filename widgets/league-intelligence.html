    Only reciprocal nonzero-score fixtures, independently shown on both official SVHL team pages, count as completed here. Unverified 0–0 schedule placeholders are excluded. Some past-winter game pages omit draw or forfeit details; the official season W/L/T remains authoritative.

    Prior winter results are shown only when official player IDs provide meaningful roster continuity. They are a separate season, not added to current standings. Brave H.C. head-to-head records come from the verified Master Workbook and HTO source, never by guessing that a team with a similar league name is the same franchise.
