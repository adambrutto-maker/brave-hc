How the shortlists will work

The workbook's AwardScoring eligibility and Rank Keys determine the preliminary top five. The separate Web Races survey asks for three nominees per award. A points leaderboard is not a substitute for award eligibility. The Hall of Fame has its own eligibility, scoring and inductee-exclusion rules.

Current public scoring and methodology have known audit items, including the Art Ross tie-break ranking and Rookie of the Year explanatory notes. These must be reconciled rather than silently patched in the web widget.
