Exact orientation convention

180° = down (coldest) · 90° = right (neutral) · 0° = up (hottest). All intermediate positions interpolate smoothly in this same coordinate system. The arrow is shorter and broader than V3.5 so it can rotate without collision.

Workbook-safe, asymmetric comparison

For this visual draft, −2σ maps to 180° and +1σ maps to 0°. This respects the asymmetry of the source's 5Arrows thresholds (−2σ, −1σ, +0.5σ, +1σ). The preview uses synthetic slider values, not any person's live projection.
