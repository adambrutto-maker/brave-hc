## Scope and boundaries

- Inspected all four *public* native Brave H.C. HTO Bulletin Board pages using a read-only request on BRUTTECH 1. The pages currently contain 10, 10, 10 and 2 posts (**32 total**), with dated titles from 2013-08-19 through 2026-10-19. Every heading had a visible posted date and title.

- This file preserves **only dates, title metadata, page-of-discovery and editorial classification**. Article body, historical fee balances, personally identifiable roster administration details and raw HTML are deliberately **not** copied to the public repository.
- Page numbers are discovery references, not immutable post permalinks; pagination can change when new posts appear. Before publishing an excerpt or linking a game, re-read the authoritative HTO source and capture an exact source-specific evidence record.
- Title classification is a **triage label**, not a verified assertion about article contents. No match to HTO game IDs is inferred from season labels or dates.
