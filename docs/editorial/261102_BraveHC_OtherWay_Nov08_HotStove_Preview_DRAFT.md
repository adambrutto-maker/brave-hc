# Braves Meet High-Scoring Other Way Looking to Reset on November 8

*Sunday, November 8, 2026 | 9:15 p.m. | Vaughan Sports Village — D Rink*

Brave H.C. returns to action Sunday against Other Way, looking to respond after a 7–2 loss to Mid Ice Crisis interrupted the momentum of its first win of the season. The Braves enter the matchup at 1–5, with 14 goals scored and 30 allowed through six games.

Other Way sits third in the eight-team Cristillo Division in the latest verified league snapshot, carrying a 2–1–2 record with 25 goals for and 23 against through five published games. Its five-goal-per-game scoring pace has come with some wide-open results: an 8–5 win over Dateline Predators on October 18 and a 7–7 tie with Sugo Sundays on October 25. That tie followed a 6–6 draw with Zamboner Crew earlier in the season.

There is more to Other Way than those high-scoring nights. The club began its schedule with a 4–3 win over Mid Ice Crisis on September 20, but it was also shut out 2–0 by Big Bastones on October 4. The variation is a reminder that a strong offensive total does not tell the whole story of a team.

### Common Opponents, Different Results

Four of Other Way's first five opponents have also appeared on the Braves' schedule, offering some useful comparisons without suggesting that one result predicts another. Other Way beat Mid Ice Crisis 4–3, while Brave H.C. lost 7–2 to the same team on November 1. The Braves beat Big Bastones 6–4 on October 25; Other Way lost 2–0 to Big Bastones three weeks earlier.

Against Zamboner Crew, Other Way played to a 6–6 tie, while Brave H.C. fell 7–1. Other Way also defeated Dateline Predators 8–5; the Braves lost 3–0 to Dateline in their season opener. Together, those results show both the offence Other Way can produce and the range of outcomes against shared competition.

### Braves Looking for a Stronger Start

Against Mid Ice Crisis, Brave H.C. trailed 4–0 after two periods before Stefano Mazzei and Adam Povegliano scored in the third. Mazzei's goal came in his first appearance of the season, and Povegliano added his second goal of the campaign. The club has now scored eight goals over its last two games, but has also allowed eleven.

The October 25 win over Big Bastones showed what the Braves can produce when they establish an early lead. A three-goal first period gave them a cushion, and Michael Provenzano's two third-period goals helped seal that 6–4 result. Against Other Way, a more complete opening two periods would give Brave H.C. a better chance to stay competitive deep into the game.

The next test comes Sunday night on D Rink. With Other Way capable of scoring in bunches and the Braves searching for consistency, the game offers an opportunity to build on the positive moments from the last two outings.
