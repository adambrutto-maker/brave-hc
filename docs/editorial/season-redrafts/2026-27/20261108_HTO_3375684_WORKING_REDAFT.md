# Braves Rally From Three Goals Down, Fall 6–5 to Other Way on Last-Second Winner

*Revised 2026–27 Hot Stove working article — Sunday, November 8, 2026*

Brave H.C. spent much of Sunday night fighting its way back into a game that appeared to be slipping away before the first intermission. Down 3–0 in the opening period, the Braves scored four of the next five goals, took their first lead in the second and twice erased a deficit. But after Stephane Saindon tied the contest at 5–5 with 1:18 remaining in the third, Other Way delivered the decisive goal with just 10 seconds left to escape with a 6–5 victory.

## Before the Puck Dropped

It was a particularly difficult result for a Brave H.C. team that had produced one of its strongest offensive performances of the young 2026–27 season. Saindon and Krishnan Rajasooriar scored twice apiece, while Jamie Rosenberg supplied the other goal. The five-goal output was second only to the six the Braves scored in their October 25 victory over Big Bastones.

Other Way arrived with a 3–1–2 record, 36 goals scored and 32 allowed through six games. Its recent results included an 8–5 victory over Dateline Predators, a 7–7 draw with Sugo Sundays and an 11–9 win against Mohawks H.C. That three-game stretch produced 26 goals for Other Way and 21 against, offering ample evidence of a team capable of both generating and conceding offence.

## How the Game Unfolded

The verified Brave H.C. historical ledger did not establish a previous meeting under the exact Other Way team name, so the strongest pregame comparison came from current-season results. Brave H.C. had beaten Big Bastones 6–4 on October 25 but lost 7–2 to Mid Ice Crisis the following Sunday. The Braves entered the contest at 1–5, having scored 14 goals and surrendered 30.

The game-night lineup featured 13 dressed skaters, including call-ups Adam Kober and Rajasooriar. Kober was making his sixth appearance of the season, while Rajasooriar was appearing for the third time. Full-time defenceman Alex Pagnotta was a game-day scratch; full-timers Anthony Paolucci and Keith Pearsoll also did not dress. Captain Adam Brutto returned to the lineup after missing the November 1 game, and Jeff Rae was in goal for his first recorded appearance with the club this season.

Other Way wasted little time establishing control of the scoreboard. Three unanswered goals at 7:00, 6:18 and 5:14 remaining in the opening period gave the visitors a 3–0 advantage.

The Braves' response came late in the period, beginning with Rajasooriar at 2:46 remaining. Assisted by Saindon and Marco Congiusti, Rajasooriar recorded his first goal of the 2026–27 season and put Brave H.C. on the board.

The comeback gained momentum in the period's closing seconds. With approximately 12 seconds remaining, Saindon scored his first of the game and second of the season, with Michael Provenzano and Mario Provenzano receiving assists.

What had been a 3–0 deficit was suddenly 3–2 at the intermission. The Braves had not erased the damage of the opening minutes, but their two late goals had transformed the position they would carry into the second period.

At 9:27 remaining, Rosenberg scored his second goal of the season, assisted by Justin Carosi, to bring the Braves level at 3–3. The goal completed the recovery from the three-goal opening-period deficit.

At 2:07 remaining, Rajasooriar struck again for his second goal of the night and second of the season. Congiusti and Carosi collected the assists, giving both players their second helpers of the game.
