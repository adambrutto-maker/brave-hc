Braves Break Through with 6–4 Win Over Big Bastones

Sunday, October 25, 2026 | Brave H.C. 6–4 Big Bastones

Brave H.C. 6, Big Bastones 4 — a three-goal opening period and two goals from Michael Provenzano carry the Braves to their first win.

Brave H.C. earned its first win of the 2026–27 season on October 25, beating Big Bastones 6–4 at Vaughan Sports Village. The Braves jumped ahead 3–0 in the first period, held on after Big Bastones closed to within a goal, and finished the night with six goals, led by Michael Provenzano's two-goal performance.

Big Bastones entered the game at 2–2, with six goals scored and eight allowed through four games. Its results included a 2–0 win over Other Way and a 4–1 loss to Mohawks HC the previous week. Brave H.C. had gone 3–2–1 in six previous regular-season meetings with Big Bastones, including a 4–2 win in October 2025.

The Braves dressed 12 full-timers and one part-timer. Marco Congiusti and Anthony Paolucci were out of the lineup, while part-timer Adam Kober made his fourth appearance of the season. Stephen Logue made his second appearance of the season in goal.

The game also marked Stephane Saindon's first appearance wearing the assistant captain's A, an honour chosen by his teammates in a team vote. He celebrated with his first goal of the season, an assist and Player of the Game recognition.

First Period — Three-Goal Start

Justin Carosi opened the scoring at 8:44 remaining with his fourth goal of the season, assisted by Keith Pearsoll. Adam Povegliano made it 2–0 at 5:04 with his first of the season, from Carosi and Francesco Triumbari. Saindon added his first of the season at 2:25, assisted by Adam Brutto and Sanjay Rajkumar, to send Brave H.C. into the intermission leading 3–0.

Second Period — Big Bastones Responds

Big Bastones scored at 7:03 remaining and again with just two seconds left, reducing the Braves' lead to 3–2 entering the third.

Third Period — Provenzano Restores the Advantage

Michael Provenzano scored at 9:20 remaining with his first goal of the season, assisted by Jamie Rosenberg and Saindon. He struck again at 6:23, his second of the game and season, with Alex Pagnotta and Kober earning the assists. The four-on-four goal put Brave H.C. up 5–2 and stood as the game-winner.

Big Bastones answered at 5:39 and 3:22 to make it 5–4. With ten seconds remaining, Rosenberg sealed the win with his first goal of the season into an empty net, assisted by Triumbari.

Game Leaders and Recognition

Provenzano led the Braves with two goals and the winner. Carosi, Saindon and Rosenberg each finished with a goal and an assist, while Povegliano scored once. Triumbari had two assists, with Pearsoll, Brutto, Rajkumar, Pagnotta and Kober adding one apiece.

Logue earned the victory with four goals against. Players of the Game were Michael Provenzano, Stephane Saindon and Stephen Logue.

After five games, the Braves stood at 1–4 with 12 goals scored and 23 allowed. The result moved their regular-season record against Big Bastones to 4–2–1 in seven games, with 24 goals for and 27 against.

Correction, November 2: An earlier version omitted assists credited to Sanjay Rajkumar and Jamie Rosenberg and misstated Adam Povegliano's points. The totals above reflect the corrected game record.
