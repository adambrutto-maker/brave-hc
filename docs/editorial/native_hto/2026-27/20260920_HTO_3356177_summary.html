Braves Open Season with 3–0 Loss to Dateline Predators

Sunday, September 20, 2026 | Brave H.C. 0–3 Dateline Predators

Brave H.C. 0, Dateline Predators 3 — the opener also marks Justin Carosi’s 300th career game.

Brave H.C. opened the 2026–27 season with a 3–0 loss to Dateline Predators on September 20. The clubs played a scoreless first period before the Predators struck twice in the second and added another in the third.

The Predators entered the season coming off a 7–15–2 winter, when they scored 45 goals and allowed 95. The Braves, meanwhile, had won eight of their previous ten regular-season meetings against Dateline, scoring 41 goals and allowing 22. Their most recent encounter had been a 4–3 Predators win in March.

The Braves dressed 14 skaters, with full-timers Marco Congiusti and Jamie Rosenberg out of the lineup. Part-timer Stefano Ardizzi made his first appearance of the season, and Dev Leong was in goal for his season debut.

First Period — Scoreless Opening

Neither team found the net in the opening period, sending the game into the first intermission tied 0–0.

Second Period — Dateline Breaks Through

Dateline scored twice in 20 seconds, with goals at 5:11 and 4:51 remaining, to take a 2–0 lead into the third.

Third Period — Predators Finish the Job

The Predators added their final goal at 8:05 remaining to complete the 3–0 result. Leong finished with three goals against in the loss.

Milestone and Recognition

Justin Carosi reached 300 career games in the season opener. It was a major personal milestone on a difficult night for the team.

Players of the Game: Justin Carosi, Michael Provenzano and Dev Leong.

Brave H.C. ended opening night at 0–1, with no goals scored and three allowed. The result also narrowed the club's regular-season advantage over Dateline Predators to 8–3 through 11 meetings.
