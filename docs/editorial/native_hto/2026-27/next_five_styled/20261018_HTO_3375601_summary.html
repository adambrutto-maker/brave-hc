Braves Fall 7–1 to Zamboner Crew After Early Lead

Sunday, October 18, 2026 | Brave H.C. 1–7 Zamboner Crew

Brave H.C. 1, Zamboner Crew 7 — Keith Pearsoll puts the Braves ahead before Zamboner Crew scores seven unanswered.

Brave H.C. jumped ahead early against Zamboner Crew on October 18, but Zamboner Crew answered with seven straight goals to win 7–1. Keith Pearsoll gave the Braves a first-period lead before Zamboner scored twice in the opening frame, three times in the second and twice in the third.

Zamboner Crew came in at 0–1–1 after a 6–6 tie with Other Way and a 6–2 loss to Mid Ice Crisis. The Braves were looking for their first win after three defeats to open the season.

Full-timers Marco Congiusti, Anthony Paolucci, Michael Provenzano and Sanjay Rajkumar were out of the lineup. Part-timer Adam Kober made his third appearance of the season, while Krishnan Rajasooriar made his second. Dev Leong was back in goal for his second appearance of the campaign.

First Period — Pearsoll Puts Braves Ahead

Pearsoll opened the scoring at 9:17 remaining with his first goal of the season, assisted by Stephane Saindon and Alex Pagnotta. Zamboner Crew tied the game at 7:06 and moved ahead at 5:10 to take a 2–1 lead into the intermission.

Second Period — Zamboner Pulls Away

Zamboner Crew scored three more goals, at 6:39, 3:05 and 2:57 remaining, extending its lead to 5–1 heading into the third.

Third Period — Final Two Goals

Zamboner Crew added a power-play goal at 7:45 remaining and another at 3:10 to complete the 7–1 result. Leong finished with seven goals against.

Game Leaders and Recognition

Pearsoll had the Braves' lone goal, while Saindon and Pagnotta picked up assists. Players of the Game were Keith Pearsoll, Jamie Rosenberg and Stephane Saindon.

The loss left the Braves at 0–4 with six goals scored and 19 allowed.
