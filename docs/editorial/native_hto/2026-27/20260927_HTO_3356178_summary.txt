Braves Fall 6–3 to Mohawks as Provenzano Reaches 50 Career Points

Sunday, September 27, 2026 | Brave H.C. 3–6 Mohawks H.C.

Brave H.C. 3, Mohawks H.C. 6 — Justin Carosi scores twice and Michael Provenzano reaches 50 career points.

Brave H.C. fell 6–3 to Mohawks H.C. on September 27 despite two goals from Justin Carosi and a third-period finish by part-timer Adam Kober. The Mohawks carried a 3–1 lead into the final period and stretched their advantage to five before the Braves scored twice late.

The Mohawks arrived after a 4–3 season-opening loss to Sugo Sundays. They were familiar opponents for Brave H.C., which entered the game 16–18–8 through 42 regular-season meetings with the Mohawks, with 161 goals for and 155 against. Their previous meeting had ended 3–2 for the Mohawks in September 2025.

Full-timers Anthony Paolucci and Adam Povegliano were out of the lineup. Part-timers Nolan Atkins, Lorcan Duffy, Adam Kober and Krishnan Rajasooriar made their first appearances of the season. Stephen Logue made his first appearance of the campaign in goal.

First Period — Mohawks Strike First

The Mohawks opened the scoring with 2:42 remaining in the first period, taking a 1–0 lead into the intermission.

Second Period — Carosi Answers

Carosi tied the game 1–1 at 9:26 remaining with his first goal of the season, assisted by Michael Provenzano and Alex Pagnotta. The Mohawks responded with two goals later in the period and headed to the third leading 3–1.

Third Period — Late Braves Goals

Three more Mohawks goals made it 6–1 before Carosi scored his second goal of the game and season at 5:30 remaining, assisted again by Provenzano. Kober added his first goal of the season at 1:20, from Henry Kong and Pagnotta, to complete the scoring at 6–3.

Game Leaders and Recognition

Carosi finished with two goals, while Provenzano and Pagnotta recorded two assists apiece. Kober supplied the other Braves goal, and Logue was charged with six goals against in his first appearance.

Provenzano's two-assist game carried an additional milestone: he reached 50 career points with Brave H.C.

Players of the Game: Justin Carosi, Adam Kober and Stephen Logue.

After two games, Brave H.C. stood at 0–2 with three goals scored and nine allowed.
