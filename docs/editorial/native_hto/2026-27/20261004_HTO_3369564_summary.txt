Braves Edged 3–2 by Michelob Morts

Sunday, October 4, 2026 | Brave H.C. 2–3 Michelob Morts

Brave H.C. 2, Michelob Morts 3 — a third-period goal decides the game after a 2–2 opening period.

Brave H.C. lost a close 3–2 decision to Michelob Morts on October 4. Twice the Braves answered a first-period deficit, but the teams were still level at 2–2 after two periods before Michelob Morts scored the only goal of the third.

Michelob Morts entered with two wins in two games, having beaten Mohawks HC 4–3 and Dateline Predators 3–1. The meeting gave the Braves a chance to test themselves against a team that had started the season strongly.

Full-timers Marco Congiusti, Anthony Paolucci, Keith Pearsoll and Stephane Saindon were out of the lineup. Part-timers Nolan Atkins and Adam Kober made their second appearances of the season, while Damian Pavaday made his season debut. Adam Carlyle made his first appearance for the Braves in goal.

First Period — Four Goals Split Evenly

Michelob Morts struck at 9:12 remaining, but Henry Kong answered at 7:10 with his first goal of the season, assisted by Kober and Pavaday. After Michelob Morts moved ahead again at 3:36, Justin Carosi tied it just seven seconds later at 3:29, scoring his third goal of the season from Michael Provenzano.

Second Period — Deadlocked

Neither team scored in the second period, keeping the contest tied 2–2 heading into the third.

Third Period — One Goal Makes the Difference

Michelob Morts took the lead at 6:38 remaining and held on for the 3–2 win. Carlyle was charged with three goals against.

Game Leaders and Recognition

Kong and Carosi supplied the Braves' goals, with Kober, Pavaday and Provenzano recording assists. Carosi's third of the season accounted for three of the club's first five goals.

Players of the Game: Justin Carosi, Henry Kong and Adam Carlyle.

Through three games, the Braves were 0–3 with five goals scored and 12 allowed.
