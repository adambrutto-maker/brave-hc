Braves Fall 7–2 to Mid Ice Crisis in November Opener

Sunday, November 1, 2026 | Brave H.C. 2–7 Mid Ice Crisis

Brave H.C. 2, Mid Ice Crisis 7 — the Braves scored twice in the third period.

Brave H.C. could not build on its first win of the season, falling 7–2 to Mid Ice Crisis on Sunday at Vaughan Sports Village. The Braves trailed 4–0 after two periods before Stefano Mazzei and Adam Povegliano scored in the third.

Mid Ice Crisis entered the game second in the eight-team Cristillo Division at 3–2, with 20 goals scored and 13 allowed through five games. The club had lost 2–1 to division-leading Mohawks HC the previous week. Against Mid Ice Crisis, the Braves had gone 1–2–2 in five previous regular-season meetings, including a 3–3 tie on December 14, 2025. Including two playoff losses, their record in the matchup stood at 1–4–2.

The Braves dressed 11 full-timers and two part-timers. Captain Adam Brutto, Anthony Paolucci and Keith Pearsoll were out of the lineup. Part-timer Adam Kober made his fifth appearance of the season, while part-timer Stefano Mazzei made his season debut. Marco Congiusti also made his first appearance of the season, and Brian Haspel made his Braves debut in goal.

First Period — Mid Ice Crisis Takes the Lead

Mid Ice Crisis opened the scoring at 9:14 remaining and carried a 1–0 lead into the intermission. A two-minute tripping penalty against Mid Ice Crisis at 6:51 gave the Braves a power-play opportunity, but they did not score.

Second Period — Mid Ice Crisis Builds a Four-Goal Advantage

Mid Ice Crisis scored three unanswered goals at 9:22, 1:16 and 0:35 remaining. The Braves went into the third period trailing 4–0.

Third Period — Two Different Finishes for the Braves

At 8:53 remaining, Stefano Mazzei streaked up the far side after a pass from Alex Pagnotta, then came in and beat the goaltender cleanly with a wrist shot. The goal, Mazzei's first goal of the game and first of the season in his season debut, put Brave H.C. on the scoreboard at 4–1 and gave Pagnotta his fifth assist of the season.

Mid Ice Crisis responded at 4:22 and 2:40 remaining, moving ahead 6–1.

At 1:47 remaining, Justin Carosi won the faceoff cleanly back to Alex Pagnotta. Adam Povegliano gathered the rebound and scored. The finish was Povegliano's first goal of the game and second of the season. Pagnotta received his sixth assist of the season, while Carosi picked up his second. The goal made it 6–2 before Mid Ice Crisis completed the scoring at 0:54 remaining for the 7–2 final.

Game Leaders and Recognition

Alex Pagnotta finished with two assists, his fifth and sixth of the season, giving him six points in six games. Mazzei and Povegliano recorded a goal apiece, while Justin Carosi had one assist and moved to four goals and two assists on the season. Brian Haspel took the loss, allowing seven goals in his first recorded appearance for the club.

Players of the Game: Alex Pagnotta, Adam Kober and Adam Povegliano.

Through six games, the Braves stand at 1–5, with 14 goals scored and 30 allowed. The result moved their regular-season record against Mid Ice Crisis to 1–3–2 in six meetings, with 15 goals for and 26 against. Including playoffs, the series stands at 1–5–2 across eight meetings, with 17 goals scored and 32 allowed.

Next Up — Other Way

Brave H.C. returns to Vaughan Sports Village — D Rink on Sunday, November 8 at 9:15 p.m. to face Other Way. Third in the Cristillo Division at 2–1–2 through five games, Other Way has scored 25 goals and allowed 23. It defeated Dateline Predators 8–5 on October 18 and tied Sugo Sundays 7–7 on October 25. Other Way also beat Mid Ice Crisis 4–3 earlier this season.

The Braves will look to return to the win column following Sunday's setback.
