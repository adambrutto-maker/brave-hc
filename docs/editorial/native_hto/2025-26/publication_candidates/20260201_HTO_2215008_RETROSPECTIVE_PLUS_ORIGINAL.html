Pearsoll Hat Trick Leads Braves to 5–1 Win Over Dateline Predators

Historical retrospective · February 1, 2026 · Regular season · Brave H.C. 5–1 Dateline Predators

Keith Pearsoll scored three times as Brave H.C. defeated Dateline Predators 5–1 on February 1, 2026. The Braves led 2–0 after the first period and carried a 3–1 advantage into the third.

Michael Provenzano put Brave H.C. ahead at 7:52 on the first-period HTO clock, assisted by Ryan Logue and Ryan Finlay. Stephane Saindon added another at 0:14, with Provenzano and Finlay earning the assists.

Dateline cut the deficit to 2–1 in the second period, but Pearsoll answered at 6:00, assisted by Mario Provenzano and Justin Carosi. The Braves entered the third ahead by two.

Pearsoll completed his hat trick with both goals in the third period. The official scoring sheet marks his goal at 1:07 as an empty-net goal and his goal at 0:51 as even-strength, with Carosi assisting on the latter. Those goals brought the final score to 5–1.

Pearsoll finished with three goals and three points. Carosi and Finlay each added two assists, while Michael Provenzano contributed a goal and an assist. The HTO goalie table credits Dev Leong with the victory and one goal against.
