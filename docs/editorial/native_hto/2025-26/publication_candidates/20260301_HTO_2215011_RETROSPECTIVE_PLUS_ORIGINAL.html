Three-Goal Third Period Lifts Dateline Past Braves, 4–3

Historical retrospective · March 1, 2026 · Regular season · Brave H.C. 3–4 Dateline Predators

Brave H.C. carried a 3–1 advantage into the third period against Dateline Predators on March 1, 2026, but conceded three unanswered goals in the final period and lost 4–3.

Justin Carosi opened the scoring at 2:36 on the first-period HTO clock, assisted by Michael Provenzano and Mark Arruda. Dateline tied the game at 0:57, sending the clubs to the first intermission level at 1–1.

The Braves took control of the scoreboard in the second. Alex Pagnotta scored an unassisted goal at 5:23, and Stephane Saindon made it 3–1 at 0:13, with Ryan Finlay and Keith Pearsoll earning the assists. Brave H.C. held that two-goal lead entering the third.

The final period belonged to Dateline on the official scoring sheet. Goals at 2:43 and 1:59 erased the deficit, and another at 0:58 put Dateline ahead 4–3. The Braves did not score in the third.

Carosi, Pagnotta and Saindon supplied the Braves' three goals. The official HTO goalie record credits Dev Leong with the loss and four goals against.
