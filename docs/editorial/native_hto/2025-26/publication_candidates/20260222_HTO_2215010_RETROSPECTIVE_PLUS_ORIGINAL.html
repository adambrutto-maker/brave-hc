Saindon and Provenzano Score Twice Each in 5–1 Win Over Royal Flushers

Historical retrospective · February 22, 2026 · Regular season · Brave H.C. 5–1 Royal Flushers

Brave H.C. defeated Royal Flushers 5–1 on February 22, 2026, behind two goals apiece from Stephane Saindon and Michael Provenzano. Justin Carosi scored the other Braves goal.

After a scoreless first period, the Braves recorded three goals in the second. Saindon opened the scoring at 5:41 on the HTO clock, assisted by Keith Pearsoll and Ryan Finlay. Provenzano followed at 4:12 from Saindon and Ryan Logue, and Carosi added another at 3:48 with Alex Pagnotta credited with the assist.

Royal Flushers answered in the same period, reducing the deficit to 3–1. The Braves then scored the only two goals of the third. Provenzano added his second at 6:22, with Carosi and Mario Provenzano assisting. Saindon completed his two-goal outing at 3:22, assisted by Logue and Mark Arruda.

Saindon finished with three points, Provenzano recorded two goals, and Logue added two assists. The official goalie record credits Justin Sadler with a win and one goal against.
