# Carosi's Third-Period Goal Earns Braves a 2–2 Tie With Mid Ice Crisis

**HISTORICAL RETROSPECTIVE — UNAPPROVED EDITORIAL REVIEW DRAFT**  
**Original game date:** March 16, 2025 · Winter 2024/2025  
**Exact HTO game ID:** 2148159 · Brave H.C. 2–2 Mid Ice Crisis  
**Official source:** [Archived HTO boxscore and original game-event table](https://www.htosports.com/teams/default.asp?u=BRAVESHOCKEY&s=hockey&sportsHQ=BRAVESHOCKEY&archTog=1&viewseas=Winter_2024%2F2025&p=boxscore&gameID=2148159)

Justin Carosi scored in the third period to bring Brave H.C. back level with Mid Ice Crisis in a 2–2 tie on March 16, 2025. The Braves led early, fell behind in the second period and found the equalizer before the final horn.

Lorcan Duffy gave Brave H.C. the opening goal at 3:12 of the first period, assisted by Mark Arruda. Mid Ice Crisis drew even on a power-play goal at 1:25, sending the teams to the intermission tied 1–1.

Mid Ice Crisis moved ahead with the only goal of the second period, scored at 3:21. Carosi answered at 6:21 of the third, with Medi Brancatella and Alex Pagnotta credited with assists, bringing the scoreboard to 2–2.

No further scoring was recorded. Matt Svec was the Braves' listed goaltender, with a tie and two goals against in the official HTO stats. The result was distinct from the teams' later March 30 and April 6 playoff meetings, which were separate games and are being researched independently.
