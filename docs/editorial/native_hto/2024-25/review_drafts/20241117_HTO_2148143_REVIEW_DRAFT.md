# Carosi Scores Twice as Braves Defeat Dateline Predators 3–1

Justin Carosi scored twice and Brave H.C. built a three-goal lead on its way to a 3–1 win over Dateline Predators on November 17, 2024. Two first-period goals provided the early separation, and a second-period insurance goal gave the Braves room to absorb a late reply.

Ryan Finlay opened the scoring at 5:58 in the first, assisted by Adam Brutto and Lorcan Duffy. Carosi added an unassisted goal at 2:12 to send Brave H.C. into the intermission ahead 2–0.

Carosi struck again at 3:50 in the second period, with Keith Pearsoll and Brutto recording the assists. Dateline Predators scored their only goal in the third, at 2:42, but could not erase the remaining two-goal deficit.

Carosi's two-goal effort and Brutto's two assists paced the recorded individual scoring, while Stephen Logue was credited with the win in goal. The result followed the Braves' earlier 6–0 victory over the same opponent on October 6, giving the team two documented wins in those autumn meetings.
