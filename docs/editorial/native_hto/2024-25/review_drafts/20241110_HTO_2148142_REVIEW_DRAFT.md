# Three-Goal Second Period Propels Brave H.C. Past Schmelts 5–3

**HISTORICAL RETROSPECTIVE — UNAPPROVED EDITORIAL REVIEW DRAFT**  
**Original game date:** November 10, 2024 · Winter 2024/2025  
**Exact HTO game ID:** 2148142 · Brave H.C. 5–3 Schmelts  
**Official source:** [Archived HTO boxscore and original game-event table](https://www.htosports.com/teams/default.asp?u=BRAVESHOCKEY&s=hockey&sportsHQ=BRAVESHOCKEY&archTog=1&viewseas=Winter_2024%2F2025&p=boxscore&gameID=2148142)

Trailing by two early in the second period, Brave H.C. answered with three consecutive goals and went on to beat Schmelts 5–3 on November 10, 2024. A productive middle frame reversed the scoreboard before the Braves finished the job with an empty-net goal late in the third.

Schmelts struck first in the opening period and added a power-play goal at 9:28 of the second to make it 2–0. Stephane Saindon started the comeback, scoring at 5:02 with Mark Arruda assisting. Medi Brancatella tied the game at 2:48 from Justin Carosi and Stefano Ardizzi, and Keith Pearsoll put the Braves ahead at 1:28 from Adam Brutto.

Mario Provenzano extended the advantage in the third period, scoring with Carosi credited with his second assist of the game. Schmelts replied to narrow the gap to 4–3, but Arruda closed out the scoring with an empty-net goal at 0:14 remaining.

Five different Braves players scored in the victory: Saindon, Brancatella, Pearsoll, Provenzano and Arruda. Dev Leong was the goaltender credited with the win, with three goals against in the official HTO statistics. The middle-period rally was the decisive swing in a game the Braves had trailed 2–0.
