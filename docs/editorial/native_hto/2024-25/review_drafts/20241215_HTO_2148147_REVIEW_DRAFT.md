Brave H.C. opened the scoring against Bears on December 15, 2024, but two third-period goals from the opposition turned a tied game into a 3–2 defeat. Stefano Mazzei and Stefano Ardizzi supplied the Braves' goals in a game that remained close to the finish.

Mazzei struck at 7:07 in the first period, assisted by Ardizzi and Neil Baghel. Bears tied the game later in the opening frame, and neither side scored in the second, setting up a 1–1 contest entering the third.

Bears scored at 8:27 and 6:10 of the third period to take a 3–1 lead. Ardizzi answered at 4:53, with Baghel and Justin Carosi credited with the assists, but the Braves could not find another equalizer.

Baghel recorded two assists, and Mazzei and Ardizzi finished with one goal apiece. Dev Leong appeared as the Brave H.C. goaltender in the official boxscore and was credited with three goals against.
