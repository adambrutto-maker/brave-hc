# Finlay Scores Twice as Brave H.C. Blanks Dateline Predators 6–0

Brave H.C. put together a six-goal performance and kept Dateline Predators off the scoresheet in a 6–0 victory on October 6, 2024. The Braves scored twice in the opening period and four more times in the second, building a lead that remained intact through the final frame.

Ryan Logue opened the scoring at the 6:18 mark of the first period, with Lorcan Duffy and Stephane Saindon credited with assists. Justin Carosi followed at 5:09, converting a play assisted by Adam Brutto to make it 2–0 before the first intermission.

The second period brought the decisive scoring run. Saindon added the third goal, assisted by Medi Brancatella and Andrew Cristillo. Ryan Finlay then scored with assists from Logue and Saindon, Marco Congiusti made it 5–0 with Brancatella and Saindon assisting, and Finlay completed his two-goal night at 1:22 with help from Saindon and Alex Pagnotta.

The final game sheet credits Stephen Logue with the win and a shutout. Ryan Finlay's pair of goals led the finishers, while Saindon contributed a goal and multiple assists. The six goals were all recorded in the first two periods; there was no scoring in the third.
