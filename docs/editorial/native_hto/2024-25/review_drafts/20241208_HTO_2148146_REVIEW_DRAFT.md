# Kober's Goal and Assist Help Braves Edge Mohawks HC 3–2

**HISTORICAL RETROSPECTIVE — UNAPPROVED EDITORIAL REVIEW DRAFT**  
**Original game date:** December 8, 2024 · Winter 2024/2025  
**Exact HTO game ID:** 2148146 · Brave H.C. 3–2 Mohawks HC  
**Official source:** [Archived HTO boxscore and original game-event table](https://www.htosports.com/teams/default.asp?u=BRAVESHOCKEY&s=hockey&sportsHQ=BRAVESHOCKEY&archTog=1&viewseas=Winter_2024%2F2025&p=boxscore&gameID=2148146)

Brave H.C. scored twice in the final stretch of the second period and held on for a 3–2 victory over Mohawks HC on December 8, 2024. Adam Kober had a goal and an assist, while Medi Brancatella supplied two assists on the goals that put the Braves in front.

Stephane Saindon opened the scoring at 7:49 of the first period. Mohawks HC responded later in the frame to leave the game tied 1–1 at the intermission.

Justin Carosi restored the advantage at 1:16 in the second, assisted by Kober and Brancatella. Just 28 seconds of game clock later, Kober added what proved to be the winning goal, with Brancatella and Alex Pagnotta credited on the play. The Braves carried a 3–1 lead into the third.

Mohawks HC scored at 6:38 of the final period to cut the margin to one, but the 3–2 score held. Matt Svec was the Brave H.C. goaltender credited with the win, allowing two goals according to the official HTO game statistics.
